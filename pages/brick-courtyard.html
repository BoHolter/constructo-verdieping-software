Residential · 2025

Brick Courtyard

A mid-terrace opened to a rebuilt courtyard — brighter living, better drainage, evenings outdoors.

Location
Utrecht
Client
Private family
Size
142 m² + courtyard
Duration
8 months
Year
2025
Budget
€285k – €310k

The brief

A 1930s mid-terrace with a dead rear yard. The family wanted a brighter kitchen, a usable courtyard for evenings, and storage that did not eat the garden.

What we built

We opened the rear wall with a glazed link, rebuilt the courtyard in brick and timber, and added a compact outdoor kitchen niche. Inside, the kitchen-diner now runs full width with underfloor heating and a flush threshold to the terrace.

Detail notes

Drainage was redesigned for heavy rain; planting beds hold runoff. Neighbour agreements were locked before steel went in, so the build stayed on schedule.

  • Full-width kitchen-diner with garden link
  • Brick courtyard and outdoor seating
  • Hidden bike / tool store
  • Improved insulation and new glazing
