Canal Townhouse

A full gut renovation of a Jordaan canal house — restored brick façade, open living floors, and quiet modern systems behind period detailing.

Location
Amsterdam Jordaan
Client
Private owners
Size
168 m²
Duration
11 months
Year
2025
Budget
€420k – €465k

The brief

The owners bought a narrow three-story canal house with great bones and a tired interior. They wanted daylight on every floor, a kitchen that faced the water, and enough structure work to future-proof the stairs and floors — without losing the street character.

What we built

Constructo restored the brick façade and canal-facing joinery, rebuilt the middle floor as an open living level, and upgraded insulation and heating behind timber linings. New black aluminium windows match the original rhythm while meeting current energy targets.

A discreet lift shaft was prepared for later install, and the attic became a quiet primary suite with a glazed dormer that stays invisible from the canal path.

Highlights

  • Façade restore with matched historic brick
  • Open-plan living floor with canal outlook
  • Structural floor reinforcement and new stair core
  • Hidden MVHR and underfloor heating
  • Primary suite in the attic with glazed dormer

Renovating a canal house?

We handle permits, heritage constraints, and a fixed scope before strip-out begins.
