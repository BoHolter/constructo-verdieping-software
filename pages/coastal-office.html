Coastal Office

An open-plan headquarters overlooking the North Sea — built for a 40-person digital studio that wanted light, calm, and low energy use.

Location
Zandvoort
Client
Tide & Co. Studio
Size
620 m²
Duration
5 months
Year
2023
Budget
€780k – €820k

The brief

Tide & Co. needed a coastal HQ that felt more like a gallery than a cubicle farm. The shell was a 1990s office with low ceilings and dated finishes. The goal: strip it back, open the floor plate, and pull daylight deep into the plan.

What we built

We removed two internal load walls (with engineered steel beams), raised the reception volume, and installed a continuous timber reception desk facing the dunes. Meeting rooms sit behind acoustic glass so the sea view stays shared.

Energy work included a heat-pump retrofit, LED throughout, and solar on the flat roof. Materials stay honest: white oiled ash, polished concrete, and recycled wool acoustic panels.

Highlights

  • Open-plan workspace for 40 desks with quiet pods
  • Curved ash reception desk made on-site
  • Triple-glazed sea-facing curtain wall upgrade
  • Heat pump + rooftop PV array
  • Two client suites with acoustic glass partitions
