Residential · 2024

Family Home

A two-story rear extension in Amstelveen that connects kitchen, garden, and upstairs bedrooms without losing the character of the original brick house.

Location
Amstelveen
Client
Family of five
Size
+94 m² extension
Duration
9 months
Year
2024
Budget
€310k – €345k

The brief

The family had outgrown a 1970s corner house. They wanted a larger kitchen-diner that opened to the garden, two extra bedrooms upstairs, and a covered patio for Dutch winters — without making the street façade look like a new build.

What we built

Constructo delivered a two-story timber-frame extension clad in matching brick and dark aluminium windows. The ground floor opens with a 6-metre sliding system; upstairs holds a primary suite and a kids’ room with built-in storage.

Landscaping was part of the contract: stepped planting beds, permeable paving, and a small rain garden that handles roof runoff. The family stayed in the front of the house for most of the build thanks to a temporary weather seal between old and new.

Highlights

  • Two-story rear extension with flush brick detailing
  • 6 m aluminium sliding doors to the garden
  • Primary suite with walk-in wardrobe
  • Covered cedar patio and outdoor kitchen stub-outs
  • Integrated landscaping and rain garden
