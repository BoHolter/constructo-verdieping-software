Frequently asked questions

Quick answers about permits, pricing, planning, and living on site during a build.

Do you handle building permits?

Yes — we advise what is needed, prepare drawings with partners when required, and submit or support your application. Timelines depend on the municipality.

How accurate is the online estimate?

It is a rough starting figure only. Final pricing always follows a site survey and a written quote with a clear scope.

Can we stay in the house during renovation?

Often yes, with temporary seals and phased work. We discuss dust, noise, and access in the kickoff plan.

What is included in a fixed price?

Everything listed in the scope document. Changes are written down and priced before we proceed — no silent extras.

How often do we get updates?

You get a named project lead and weekly progress notes (and photos) while the site is active.

Which areas do you cover?

Amsterdam and the wider Randstad. Further out is possible for larger projects — ask us.

Ready to build?

Tell us about your project — renovations, new builds, or commercial fit-outs.
