Garden Pavilion

A freestanding timber-and-glass studio at the end of a Haarlem garden — workspace by day, guest room by night, built to feel like part of the landscape.

Location
Haarlem
Client
Couple + home office
Size
38 m² pavilion
Duration
4 months
Year
2024
Budget
€95k – €110k

The brief

Working from home had taken over the dining table. The clients wanted a quiet backyard pavilion with power, fibre, and a pull-out daybed for guests — without eating the whole garden or triggering a heavy permit path.

What we built

Constructo delivered a prefabricated timber frame clad in vertical cedar, with triple glazing on the garden side and a solid rear wall for neighbours. A gravel patio and low planting beds stitch the pavilion back to the main house path.

Services run underground from the house: electrics, data, and a compact heat pump. The roof carries sedum for runoff and a cleaner skyline from the upstairs bedrooms.

Highlights

  • Prefabricated timber frame, assembled on site in five days
  • Full-height garden glazing with hidden blinds
  • Integrated desk wall and guest daybed niche
  • Sedum roof and permeable patio
  • Permit-light footprint under local rules
