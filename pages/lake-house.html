Lake House

A year-round waterside home with open living, guest suite, and a rebuilt dock.

Location
Loosdrecht
Client
Couple
Size
186 m²
Duration
10 months
Year
2024
Budget
€510k – €560k

The brief

Replace a tired weekend cabin with a year-round lake house: open living to the water, a guest suite, and a dock that survives winter storms.

What we built

Timber-frame structure on upgraded foundations, triple glazing facing the lake, and a covered terrace. Services were routed for heat pump and future EV charging at the driveway.

Detail notes

Flood-aware detailing and corrosion-resistant fixings were specified for the dock. The guest suite can close off for privacy without losing the main view corridor.

  • Lake-facing living with flush threshold
  • Guest suite and covered terrace
  • Rebuilt timber dock
  • Heat pump ready plant room

We plan structure, moisture, and permits before the first pile goes in.
