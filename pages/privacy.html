Privacy policy

How Constructo handles personal data on this prototype website. Last updated: October 2026.

What we collect

If you use the contact form, we may process your name, email, phone number, project details, and any files you attach. Theme preference may be stored in your browser (localStorage).

Why we use it

To respond to enquiries, prepare quotes, and improve the site experience. This demo does not send data to a live server — submissions stay in your browser session only.

Sharing

We do not sell personal data. In a live environment, data would only be shared with processors needed to run email or hosting, under contract.

Your rights

You can ask for access, correction, or deletion of your data by emailing info@constructo.example. For this prototype, clearing site data in your browser removes locally stored preferences.
