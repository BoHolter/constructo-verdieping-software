Rooftop Terrace

Turning a leaky flat roof in Oud-West into a proper outdoor room — waterproofed, furnished for evenings, and opened to the living space with new sliding glass.

Location
Amsterdam Oud-West
Client
Apartment owners
Size
52 m² terrace
Duration
5 months
Year
2025
Budget
€145k – €165k

The brief

The top-floor flat had a cracked bitumen roof and a single door that barely opened. The owners wanted a terrace they could use year-round evenings: seating, planting, and a weather-tight connection to the living room — without flooding the neighbours below.

What we built

Constructo stripped the old roof, upgraded insulation and waterproofing, and laid a raised deck with discreet drainage. A glass balustrade keeps views open; planters and a pergola frame create shelter without blocking light into the flat.

Inside, we widened the opening with a three-panel sliding system and rebuilt the threshold so indoor and outdoor floors align. Lighting and outdoor power were part of the same package.

Highlights

  • Full roof rebuild with certified waterproofing
  • Raised deck and hidden drainage channels
  • Glass balustrade and soft outdoor lighting
  • Three-panel sliding doors flush with the floor
  • Planter frames and pergola for wind shelter
