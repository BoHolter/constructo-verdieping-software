Urban Loft Revamp

A warehouse conversion in Amsterdam-Noord turned into a bright live-work loft with custom millwork and open steel framing.

Location
Amsterdam-Noord
Client
Private residence
Size
186 m²
Duration
7 months
Year
2024
Budget
€420k – €460k

The brief

The owners bought a raw former print workshop and wanted a loft that kept the industrial bones — brick, beams, and arched windows — while adding a calm kitchen, sleeping mezzanine, and a quiet studio corner for remote work.

What we built

Constructo handled structural reinforcement of the mezzanine, new insulated glazing in the existing arches, a full electrical upgrade, and a kitchen island with custom oak millwork. Underfloor heating was installed on the ground level without raising the finished floor height beyond 40 mm.

We coordinated the steel stair fabricator on-site so the open tread design lined up with the existing beam grid. Acoustical baffles above the living zone keep the hard surfaces from echoing while staying visually light.

Highlights

  • Mezzanine bedroom with freestanding steel balustrade
  • Bespoke oak kitchen and storage wall
  • Retained arched warehouse windows with secondary glazing
  • Hidden utility room behind sliding panel system
  • LED track lighting integrated into existing beams
