Warehouse Loft

A warehouse bay turned into a live-work loft with mezzanine and daylight studio.

Location
Amsterdam Noord
Client
Creative studio
Size
220 m²
Duration
9 months
Year
2025
Budget
€390k – €430k

The brief

Turn a former warehouse bay into a live-work loft: double-height living, a quiet mezzanine bedroom, and a daylight studio corner for shoots.

What we built

We kept the brick shell, inserted a steel mezzanine, upgraded the roof lights, and built a kitchen volume that anchors the plan. Acoustic lining keeps neighbours happy when the studio works late.

Detail notes

Fire strategy and escape routes were designed with the building owner. Exposed services stay readable but tidy — part of the industrial character, not leftover chaos.

  • Steel mezzanine and double-height living
  • Daylight studio corner
  • Acoustic upgrades
  • Preserved brick and steel windows
